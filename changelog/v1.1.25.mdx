`v1.1.25` · Changes since `v1.1.24`.

### Improved

- **Checkbox staging in File Status** — Each file's checkbox now stages or unstages it directly, replacing the separate selection checkbox and the `+`/`−` row buttons. A folder's checkbox in Tree mode stages or unstages all its files, and the **Changed** and **Staged** header checkboxes stage or unstage everything. **Stage selected** and **Unstage selected** appear in the section header when you `Cmd`- or `Shift`-click two or more files. See [Commit](/docs/commit#staging).
